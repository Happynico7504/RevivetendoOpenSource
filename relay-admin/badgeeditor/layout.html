Before you submit

  • Make sure the badges can actually be won: not sealed in, not stacked inside obstacles.
  • An admin reviews every layout before it can appear in the arcade; they may reject it with a note.
  • While it waits for review (and once approved) it can't be edited — “Save as copy” to make changes.
